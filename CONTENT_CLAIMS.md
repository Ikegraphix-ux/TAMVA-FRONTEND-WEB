# Public claims register

This register is the source of truth for public claims on TAMVA's website. A claim may be published only when its exact approved wording, evidence, owner and review date are recorded here.

## Release gate

Before institutional or public production use, assign an owner and review date for each claim that will appear publicly. Add source links or implementation evidence, and copy the approved wording into the relevant page. Claims without evidence remain unpublished.
